Who we are

About Lemak Talent Solutions

LEMAK TALENT SOLUTIONS LIMITED is a Kenyan private limited company established to provide professional talent acquisition, recruitment and human resource solutions to organisations seeking to attract, develop and retain competent and reliable employees.

We are committed to connecting organisations with suitable talent while supporting individuals in identifying meaningful employment and career opportunities.

Incorporated under the Companies Act, 2015 of Kenya, we comply with applicable Kenyan laws, regulations and professional requirements.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and leading talent solutions partner in Kenya and across Africa, recognised for connecting organisations with exceptional people and creating sustainable employment opportunities.

Our Mission

To provide professional, ethical and technology-enabled recruitment and human resource solutions that connect organisations with competent, reliable talent and help individuals build meaningful careers.

Frequently asked

Questions we are often asked

What services does Lemak Talent Solutions offer?

Recruitment and talent acquisition, human resource consultancy, staff outsourcing and workforce solutions, training and capacity building, payroll and HR administration, and career and employment support.

Which organisations do you work with?

Private companies and SMEs, NGOs, financial institutions, manufacturing and construction companies, hospitality and healthcare organisations, educational institutions, government-related and international organisations, and start-ups.

How does your recruitment process work?

We follow seven steps: Understand, Source, Screen, Assess, Match, Place and Support, always starting with the unique requirements of each client.

Do you provide payroll and HR administration?

Yes. We offer payroll administration support, employee records management, HR documentation, statutory deductions support, and leave and attendance administration.

Is client and candidate information kept confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is one of our core values, and we handle client and candidate information responsibly.

Where is Lemak Talent Solutions located?

Nextgen Mall, 5th Floor, Room No. 38, Mombasa Road, Nairobi, Kenya. We intend to expand progressively across East Africa.
