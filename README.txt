Lemak Talent Solutions website - deployment
1. Upload everything in this folder to the web root of lemaktalent.com (index.html, images/, robots.txt, sitemap.xml).
2. Submit https://lemaktalent.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
3. Contact form opens the visitor's email app addressed to info@lemaktalent.com. For direct submissions connect a form service (e.g. Formspree) later.
4. Confirm the social handles in index.html (JSON-LD sameAs and footer icons) are your live accounts.
